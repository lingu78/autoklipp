### Description

```
Autoklipp turns the photos and videos on your iPhone into a finished film, cut to the beat of the music – any length from 15 seconds to 10 minutes, made entirely on your iPhone.

PICK, CHOOSE, DONE
• Pick up to 200 photos and videos straight from Photos.
• Choose a length from 15 seconds to 10 minutes, or let Autoklipp suggest one.
• Choose a style and a song, and get a finished film with transitions and music.

A FILM THAT FEELS EDITED
• Cuts land on the beat of the music.
• The best moments are picked for you: sharp shots, faces and steady video rather than blurry or shaky ones.
• Photos come alive with slow pans and zooms.
• Five styles – Calm, Energetic, Nostalgic, Travel and Cinematic – each with its own pace, transitions and colours.
• Built-in music made for Autoklipp and free to use in your films, or your own audio file.
• Keep the sound from your videos: the music is lowered while people talk or cheer.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Reorder, remove or lock clips in the clip strip; the film is remade around your choices.
• Try another version with one tap.
• Add a title and an end credit.
• Describe the film you want – "a calm film about the summer, focusing on the children" – and Apple Intelligence picks and orders the clips on your iPhone. (Requires Apple Intelligence.)

SAVE AND SHARE
• Export in 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1, in 1080p or 4K.
• Save to Photos or share straight away.
• Your films are kept, so you can come back and change them.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
On the inner display your film plays on the left while the steps sit on the right. Fold or unfold at any time: the film keeps playing and nothing you chose is lost. On the outer display the controls move to the side, leaving the height to your film.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, and Autoklipp collects no data.
```

## App Review – Notes

```
Autoklipp needs no account and no network connection; everything runs on the device.

To try it: tap New film, pick a few photos and videos, choose a length (for example 30 sec), choose a style and a song, and tap Create film. The finished film plays with a clip strip under it (drag to reorder, swipe up to remove, touch and hold to lock). Tap Export to save it to Photos or share it.

"Describe your film" uses Apple Intelligence on the device (Foundation Models) and appears only where Apple Intelligence is available; without it the app works the same, without the description.

All built-in music was composed for the app and is dedicated to the public domain (CC0), so films made with it can be shared freely.

On iPhone Duo the app shows two columns on the inner display and keeps playing when the phone is folded or unfolded.
```
